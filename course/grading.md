# Grading and Working Rubrics

The syllabus establishes the following weights:

| Weight | Category |
|---:|---|
| 30% | Asynchronous Participation |
| 30% | Projects 1–4 |
| 40% | Project 5: Capstone Synthesis |

Projects 1–4 are equally weighted within their 30% category unless the instructor announces otherwise in UBLearns.

## General Project Criteria

Across the course, project work is evaluated for:

- **Ontological judgment:** distinctions and commitments are defensible.
- **Scope and purpose:** the model or analysis answers the stated problem rather than expanding indiscriminately.
- **Explicitness:** assumptions and modeling decisions are stated rather than hidden.
- **Logical quality:** OWL or other formal artifacts are used correctly where required.
- **Validation:** reasoner, SHACL, comparison, or other checks are interpreted correctly.
- **Definitions and documentation:** terminology is clear and agrees with the model.
- **Revision and reflection:** feedback and tool output are evaluated rather than accepted mechanically.

Each project README contains assignment-specific criteria.

## Participation Criteria

Peer reviews and debate reflections are evaluated for substantive engagement, charitable reconstruction, reasoning, specificity, and usefulness. Completion alone does not guarantee full credit when a response does not engage the course material.
