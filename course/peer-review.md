# Pull-Request Peer Review

Peer review is required for Projects 1–4.

## Minimum requirement

Review at least one classmate’s pull request for each of Projects 1–4.

## Required review structure

Use the following headings in your GitHub review:

```markdown
## Strength
Identify one substantive thing the submission does well and explain why it matters.

## Question or Concern
Identify one modeling, conceptual, logical, methodological, or presentation issue worth considering. Explain the reasoning behind the concern.

## Recommendation
Propose a concrete improvement, or explain why the concern does not require a revision.
```

## What counts as substantive

Good comments engage the project’s actual commitments. Depending on the assignment, useful questions include:

- Is the scope appropriate to the competency question?
- Is a class really a repeatable type rather than a named individual?
- Is a role being confused with its bearer?
- Is information being confused with what it is about?
- Is a relation sufficiently clear and appropriately constrained?
- Does the textual definition agree with the OWL axioms?
- Does the reasoner result support the conclusion being claimed?
- Is an LLM output being treated as evidence rather than as a proposal to evaluate?
- Does a validation result establish consistency, conformance, possibility, or actual entailment?

## Review etiquette

Be charitable, specific, and useful. Avoid grading your classmate’s work. The instructor assigns grades.
