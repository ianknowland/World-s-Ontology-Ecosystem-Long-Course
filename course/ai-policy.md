# Course Policy on Artificial Intelligence

AI tools may be used when an assignment explicitly permits or requires them. Because evaluating and using AI systems is part of the subject matter of this course, Projects 3–5 include required or permitted interaction with large language models or other AI tools.

Students remain responsible for the accuracy, reasoning, modeling choices, and ontology artifacts they submit. AI-generated content must be evaluated rather than treated as authoritative.

## Disclosure

When substantive AI assistance is used, include an `AI Use` section in the project notes identifying:

- the tool/model used;
- what task it was used for;
- the important prompts or instructions provided;
- what output was accepted, rejected, or modified; and
- any material limitations encountered.

The assignment may require a more detailed prompt-and-response log.
