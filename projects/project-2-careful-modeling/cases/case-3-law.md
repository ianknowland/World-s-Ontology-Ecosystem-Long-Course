# Case 3 — Modeling Law

## Scenario

A regulation requires a licensed operator to submit an annual compliance report. A particular operator bears the licensed-operator role during 2026. The regulator publishes a document containing the requirement. The operator later performs an act of submitting Report R-26, and the submission is evaluated for whether it satisfies the requirement.

## Competency questions

- What entity contains or expresses the requirement?
- Who bears the licensed-operator role?
- What act is required?
- Which particular act of submission occurred?
- Which report was submitted?
- How should satisfaction or violation of the requirement be represented without treating the requirement itself as the action?

Distinguish normative information, role bearers, acts, documents, and evaluation results.
