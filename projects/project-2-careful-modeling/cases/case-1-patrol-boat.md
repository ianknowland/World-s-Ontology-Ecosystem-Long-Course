# Case 1 — Patrol Boat Motion and Measurement

## Competency question

At what speed does a patrol boat move, measured in knots, during a one-hour interval?

Represent at minimum:

- a particular patrol boat;
- an act of motion in which the boat participates;
- speed and duration measurement data about that motion;
- the values 12.0 and 1.0;
- the units knot and hour; and
- a class that allows a reasoner to infer when a patrol boat is a `MovingPatrolBoat`.

Your model must preserve the distinction between the motion process and information about the motion. Include one deliberate category-error test, explain why the ontology becomes inconsistent, and repair it without weakening the relevant upper-level distinctions.
