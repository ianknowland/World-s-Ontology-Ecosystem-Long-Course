# Debate Reflection 1 — Lecture Set 1

Post your response in the instructor-created GitHub Discussion for Lecture Set 1.

Focus on **one substantive disagreement** in the recording.

## 1. Issue Under Dispute

State the question or proposition on which the disagreement turns.

## 2. Key Assumption or Point of Disagreement

Identify the distinction, premise, modeling principle, evidential standard, or practical consideration that explains why the positions diverge.

## 3. Your Assessment

Defend your own reasoned assessment. You may agree with either position, reject both, or defend a conditional position.

## 4. What Could Change Your Mind?

Identify evidence, an argument, a counterexample, a formal result, or a practical consideration that would materially change your assessment.
