# Course Repository Conduct

This repository is a classroom and professional learning environment. Technical disagreement is expected and encouraged; personal attacks are not.

Students should:

- criticize models, arguments, definitions, axioms, and workflows rather than people;
- represent a classmate’s position charitably before criticizing it;
- make peer reviews specific and actionable;
- avoid posting confidential, private, export-controlled, proprietary, or otherwise restricted material;
- avoid posting personal information about themselves or others;
- follow University at Buffalo academic-integrity and student-conduct requirements.

Questions about course conduct should be directed to the instructors through the course communication channel identified in UBLearns.
